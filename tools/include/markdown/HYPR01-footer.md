=== "Display Manager"

    Hyprland uses **SDDM** as its default display manager.

=== "Session files"

    - `/usr/share/wayland-sessions/hyprland-uwsm.desktop`

=== "Configuration"

    - `/etc/xdg/hypr/armbian.conf`: Armbian defaults, replaced on update
    - `~/.config/hypr/hyprland.conf`: your settings, sourced after the defaults

=== "Autologin"

    Autologin configuration is stored in:

    - `/etc/sddm.conf.d/autologin.conf`
